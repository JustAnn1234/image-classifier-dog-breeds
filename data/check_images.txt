Questions regarding Uploaded Image Classification:

1. Did the three model architectures classify the breed of dog in Dog_01.jpg to be the same breed? If not, report the differences in the classifications.

Answer: Yes, all three model architectures (ResNet, AlexNet, and VGG) classified the breed of dog in Dog_01.jpg as a golden retriever.


2. Did each of the three model architectures classify the breed of dog in Dog_01.jpg to be the same breed of dog as that model architecture classified Dog_02.jpg? If not, report the differences in the classifications.

Answer: ResNet and VGG classified Dog_02.jpg as a golden retriever, matching their classification of Dog_01.jpg. AlexNet failed to classify Dog_02.jpg as the same breed, instead misclassifying the rotated image as a proboscis monkey (nasalis larvatus).


3. Did the three model architectures correctly classify Animal_Name_01.jpg and Object_Name_01.jpg to not be dogs? If not, report the misclassifications.

Answer: Yes, all three model architectures correctly classified Frog_01.jpg and Coffee_mug_01.jpg as not dogs (PetLabelDog: 0 and ClassLabelDog: 0).


4. Based upon your answers for questions 1. - 3. above, select the model architecture that you feel did the best at classifying the four uploaded images. Describe why you selected that model architecture as the best on uploaded image classification.

Answer: Either ResNet or VGG performed the best. Both models achieved 100% accuracy on classifying dogs vs. non-dogs, correctly identified the non-dog images (Frog_01.jpg and Coffee_mug_01.jpg), and correctly identified both Dog_01.jpg and Dog_02.jpg as golden retrievers regardless of image rotation. AlexNet performed worse because the orientation change in Dog_02.jpg caused it to misclassify the dog as a proboscis monkey.
